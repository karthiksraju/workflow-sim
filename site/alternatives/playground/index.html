Illustrative demo. Nothing runs in your browser. It animates the billing example that ships with workflow-sim. The real tool runs your Python in a fresh worker process.

In the real thing, three parts are yours.

Your real code

The asyncio or Celery workflow you already ship. Each run starts in a fresh worker process.

Your model of the world

You model the services it calls. Time is virtual, and failures land exactly when you schedule them.

Your checks

Assertions about final state. PASS means they held in that modeled run. It won't write the fix; it tells you whether yours held.
