Failure testing for Python asyncio & Celery workflows

The response was lost. The receipt wasn't.

When a retry meets a lost acknowledgement, good code quietly does things twice. workflow-sim runs your real workflow against modeled services, in virtual time, with the failures you choose, then checks what was actually written.

The method, in the order it happens

Your real code.

The asyncio or Celery workflow you already ship, unchanged, in a fresh worker process for every run.

A world you model.

You describe the services it talks to. Time is virtual, and the failures arrive exactly when you say.

A verdict on what was left behind.

You write checks against final state: receipts, rows, alerts. PASS means they held in that modeled run. It won't write the fix, and it doesn't certify production. It tells you whether your fix held up.

Six shipped examplessynthetic, modeled services

  1. Payments

    The receipt commits, the acknowledgement doesn't, and the webhook arrives twice.

  2. Order fulfillment

    Payment declines after stock is reserved. Release only this order's stock.

  3. Imports

    Interrupted between the row writes and the checkpoint. No duplicate rows after recovery.

  4. Documents & AI

    One extraction retries, another finishes late. Every chunk, in order, before publishing.

  5. Monitoring

    Recovery and a new outage land inside the alert delay. Only the current incident alerts.

  6. Meetings

    A new recording finishes before a stale one. The new content survives.
